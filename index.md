## Project Showcase

### Bike Sharing Demand Analysis

**Research question:** How do time of day, weather conditions, and temperature relate to bike-sharing demand in Washington, D.C.?

**Dataset:** [UCI Bike Sharing Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/275/bike+sharing+dataset)

**Methods:** I used Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to inspect the dataset, check missing values, calculate descriptive statistics, and visualize hourly demand, weather-related differences, and the relationship between normalized temperature and rental counts.

**Key findings:** The hourly demand chart helps identify changes in rental activity throughout the day. The weather comparison shows differences in average rentals across weather situations. The temperature scatter plot illustrates the variation in rental demand across normalized temperature values.

**Limitation:** This is an exploratory analysis. The observed relationships do not establish causation, and other factors such as season, working days, and holidays may also affect demand.

**Project status:** Ongoing
